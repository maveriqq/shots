# Idea bank

Ideas researched but not built yet. Sources are starting points, not products to copy.

## Pylon — telecom network operations
- **Pain:** network and ops teams drown in alerts; most are noise and the real incident gets lost.
- **Concept:** AI folds thousands of alerts into a few incidents, with a map of affected customers and a likely cause.
- **Select line:** 3,400 nodes and 1,200 alerts an hour → incidents with blast radius and cause → the on-call engineer sees 3 real incidents instead of a wall of alarms.
- **Key moment:** an avalanche of alerts collapses into three incident cards while the affected area spreads on the map.
- **Note:** the pain is well confirmed, but AIOps is a crowded space; the visual has to carry it.
- **Sources:** r/sysadmin and r/devops threads on alert fatigue; NOC alert-fatigue articles.

## Counterpoint — research
- **Pain:** researchers look for papers that support a hypothesis and miss the ones that contradict it until review.
- **Concept:** not just “find contrasting papers” (existing tools already do that), but explain *why* papers disagree — different populations, metrics or time periods — and group the citation graph by those reasons.
- **Key moment:** a tangle of conflicting papers sorts itself into clusters labelled by the reason they disagree.
- **Sources:** literature-review guides on apparent contradictions between studies; existing citation-context tools.
